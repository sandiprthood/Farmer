🌱 How to Farm – Wheat

Basic step-by-step farming guidance

Sowing

November

Harvest

March–April

Duration

Approximately 4–6 months

1️⃣ Soil Preparation

Prepare a fine seedbed, remove weeds and use soil-test recommendations for nutrient management.

2️⃣ Seed Selection

Use certified seed suitable for your local climate and recommended sowing period.

3️⃣ Irrigation

Schedule irrigation according to crop stage, soil moisture and rainfall. Avoid unnecessary watering.

4️⃣ Fertilizer

Use soil-test-based fertilizer recommendations rather than applying the same quantity to every field.

5️⃣ Pest Monitoring

Regularly inspect plants and use integrated pest management.

6️⃣ Harvest

Harvest when the crop reaches appropriate maturity and store the grain in a clean, dry environment.

🚱 Low Rain & Drought Solutions

💧 Efficient Irrigation

Use efficient irrigation systems where suitable and schedule irrigation based on crop need.

🌾 Mulching

Mulch can reduce soil evaporation and help conserve moisture.

🌱 Drought-Tolerant Crops

Consider crops such as pearl millet, sorghum and other locally recommended drought-tolerant varieties.

🏞️ Rainwater Harvesting

Farm ponds and suitable water-harvesting structures can help store rainfall for later use.
