🌱 How to Farm – Wheat

Basic step-by-step farming guidance

Sowing

November

Harvest

March–April

Duration

Approximately 4–6 months

1️⃣ Soil Preparation

Prepare a fine seedbed, remove weeds and use soil-test recommendations for nutrient management.

2️⃣ Seed Selection

Use certified seed suitable for your local climate and recommended sowing period.

3️⃣ Irrigation

Schedule irrigation according to crop stage, soil moisture and rainfall.

4️⃣ Fertilizer

Use soil-test-based fertilizer recommendations.

5️⃣ Pest Monitoring

Regularly inspect plants and use integrated pest management.

6️⃣ Harvest

Harvest when the crop reaches appropriate maturity and store grain in a clean, dry environment.
