🌱 How to Farm

Complete step-by-step farming guide

Crop: 🌾 Wheat

Sowing Season
November
Harvest Season
April
Duration
5-6 Months

Step-by-Step Process:

1

🌍 Soil Preparation

Plow the field 3-4 times to break hard soil. Add compost or manure. Level the field for uniform irrigation. Check soil pH (6-7 is best).

2

🌰 Seed Selection & Treatment

Choose certified seeds (DBW 187, PBW 550 varieties). Treat seeds with Trichoderma to prevent diseases. Use 100 kg seeds per acre.

3

💧 Irrigation Method

1st Irrigation: 20-25 days after sowing. 2nd: 40-45 days. 3rd: 60-65 days. Use drip irrigation if available for water saving.

4

🧪 Fertilizer Application

Basal: 30-30-15 NPK. Top dressing: 40 kg N at 30 days. Foliar spray: 2% DAP at 60 days.

5

🐛 Pest Management

Watch for aphids, armyworms. Use Neem oil spray (5%) or spray Imidacloprid (17.8 SL) at early sign.

6

🌾 Harvesting

Harvest when leaves turn yellow and grains are hard. Use combine harvester or manual cutting. Store in dry place.

🌾 Crop Cutting & Harvest Guide

Know when and how to harvest your crop

🍚 RICE

⏱️ Harvesting Time
3–4 months after sowing
👀 Ready to Harvest Signs
Yellow/golden leaves, dry grains, grain falls easily from stalk
✂️ Harvesting Method
Use sickle or combine harvester. Cut 15-20 cm above ground.
💧 Moisture Content
13-14% (optimal for storage)

🌾 WHEAT

⏱️ Harvesting Time
5–6 months after sowing
👀 Ready to Harvest Signs
Ears bend down, grain is hard, straw turns brown
✂️ Harvesting Method
Use combine harvester for efficient harvesting

🧶 COTTON

⏱️ Harvesting Time
3–4 months after flowering
👀 Ready to Harvest Signs
Bolls open, cotton is fluffy and white
✂️ Harvesting Method
Manual picking or mechanical picker

🌿 Nutrients & Fertilizer Guide

NPK guide for healthy crops

🟡 NITROGEN (N)

Function
Essential for leaf and stem growth
Sources
Urea (46%), Ammonium Sulfate
Dosage (per acre)
50-100 kg (depends on crop)
Deficiency Signs
Yellowing leaves, stunted growth

🟣 PHOSPHORUS (P)

Function
Promotes root and fruit development
Sources
DAP (18-46), SSP (16% P)
Dosage (per acre)
20-30 kg P₂O₅
Deficiency Signs
Purple leaves, poor root growth

⚫ POTASSIUM (K)

Function
Strengthens plant immunity & fruit quality
Sources
MOP (60% K), SOP (50% K)
Dosage (per acre)
15-20 kg K₂O
Deficiency Signs
Leaf edges brown, weak stems

🚱 Low Rain & Drought Solutions

What to do when rainfall is insufficient

💡 Water Management Strategies

1

💧 Drip Irrigation

Save 30-40% water. Apply water directly to plant roots. Perfect for dry regions.

2

🌾 Mulching

Cover soil with straw/leaves. Reduces evaporation by 50%. Keeps soil cool and moist.

3

🚜 Zero Tillage

No plowing. Retains soil moisture. Plant directly in previous crop residue.

4

💨 Windbreaks

Plant trees around fields. Reduces wind speed & water loss by evaporation.

🌱 Drought-Resistant Crops

Best Option
🌾 Bajra (Pearl Millet) • 🌾 Jowar • 🥜 Groundnut
Why Choose These?
Needs minimal water, can survive in dry climate
Yield Expected
10-15 Q/acre even with low rainfall

💾 Water Harvesting Techniques

🏞️ Build Check Dams

Small barriers in streams to collect rainwater. Use during dry season.

🏊 Dig Farm Ponds

1 pond per 2-5 acres. Store rainwater for irrigation in summer.

⬇️ Inject Deep Wells

Access groundwater. Install bore wells during monsoon planning.

🎯 Government Support

📱 Check for drought relief schemes in your state. Apply for crop insurance. Get subsidies on drip irrigation equipment (up to 50%).
